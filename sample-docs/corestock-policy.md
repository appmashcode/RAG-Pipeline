# CoreStock Inventory Management — Company Handbook

## 1. Leave Policy

Har full-time employee ko saal me 22 paid annual leaves milti hain. Ye leaves
1 January ko reset hoti hain aur maximum 8 din agle saal carry forward ho sakte hain.
Sick leave alag se 10 din per year hai aur uske liye medical certificate 3 din se
zyada absence par lazmi hai.

Maternity leave 16 hafte ki hai aur paternity leave 2 hafte ki. Unpaid leave ke liye
manager ki tehreeri (written) approval chahiye hoti hai.

## 2. Working Hours aur Remote Work

Office timing Monday se Friday 9:00 AM se 6:00 PM hai, jis me 1 ghanta lunch break
shamil hai. Har employee hafte me 2 din remote kaam kar sakta hai, lekin Tuesday
mandatory office day hai kyunki us din sprint planning hoti hai.

Overtime approval ke baad hi count hota hai aur uska rate normal hourly rate ka
1.5 guna hai.

## 3. Inventory Reorder Rules

Warehouse me har SKU ka reorder point formula ye hai:

    Reorder Point = (Average Daily Sales x Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock

Safety stock ka default 15% rakha gaya hai. Agar kisi SKU ki demand variability
30% se zyada ho to safety stock barha kar 25% kar diya jata hai.

Purchase order tab automatically generate hota hai jab stock level reorder point se
neeche chala jaye. Standard supplier lead time 12 din hai, magar imported items ke
liye 28 din hai.

## 4. Stock Audit

Full physical stock audit har quarter ke aakhri hafte me hota hai. Cycle counting
har hafte hoti hai jis me A-category items (top 20% by value) cover kiye jate hain.

Agar physical count aur system count me 2% se zyada farq aaye to Inventory Manager
ko within 24 hours escalate karna zaroori hai.

## 5. Damaged aur Expired Stock

Damaged stock ko "QUARANTINE" location me move kiya jata hai aur 7 din ke andar
supplier claim file karni hoti hai. Expired stock monthly write-off cycle me
dispose hota hai, aur uski approval Finance Head deta hai.

Write-off ki limit per month PKR 250,000 hai. Is se zyada amount ke liye CEO
approval darkar hoti hai.

## 6. Vendor Payment Terms

Standard payment term Net 30 hai. Jo vendors 3 saal se zyada purane hain unke liye
Net 45 available hai. Early payment par 2% discount milta hai agar 10 din ke andar
payment ho jaye.

Invoice #A-4471 wala dispute abhi tak pending hai aur uska resolution owner
Finance Manager hai.
